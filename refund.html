Return Policy

Last updated: 10.06.2026

Thank you for shopping at corse.ge. We want to ensure you have a great shopping experience. Please read our policy regarding returns and refunds carefully.

1. Returns

  • You have 14 calendar days to return an item from the date you received it (under Georgian Consumer Rights Law).
  • To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused, undamaged, and in the same condition that you received it. It must also be in the original packaging.
  • Certain items (such as personalized goods, cosmetics, or intimate items for hygiene reasons) may not be eligible for return.

2. Refunds

  • Once we receive your returned item, we will inspect it and notify you that we have received it.
  • If your return is approved, we will initiate a refund to your original method of payment.
Important Note on Bank Refunds: Refunds will be credited back to the original credit/debit card used for the purchase through the Bank of Georgia system. Please note that it may take a few business days for the credit to appear on your account, depending on your bank's policies.

3. Shipping Costs for Returns

  • You will be responsible for paying your own shipping costs for returning your item, unless the return is due to our error (e.g., wrong or defective item delivered).
  • Shipping costs are non-refundable.

4. Cancellation of Orders

You can cancel your order before it has been shipped. Please contact our support team immediately at the email below to request a cancellation. If the order has already been dispatched, the standard return policy will apply.
